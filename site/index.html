The catch

Their lakes are running out of water.

Years of drought and more demand than the rivers can supply have drained America's two biggest reservoirs to about a quarter full. When the water drops, the fish lose their homes first.

Who's behind this

Fish Date is a self-funded awareness project from Heretic, a creative studio in Los Angeles. No ads, no data selling, no actual fish dating.

FAQ
Is this a dating site?
No. It's a parody about America's lakes losing water. There are no sign-ups, no real people and no matches. Not affiliated with any dating service.
Is it affiliated with any dating app?
No. Fish Date is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any dating app or dating service.
Where does the money go?
Straight to the lake groups, through their own pages. Fish Date and Heretic never collect or hold donations.
Why fish?
"Save the watershed" doesn't stop traffic. A sunfish in a tie does.
Is Fish Date a nonprofit?
No. It's a self-funded awareness project from Heretic. Donations go directly to independent nonprofits.
Do you collect my information?
No. This site has no forms, no accounts and no trackers. The fish chats are pre-written, and nothing you tap is saved.
